# Surface brief — CREDENCE dashboard (all 5 areas)

Scope: the whole application shell + Overview, Portfolio, Borrowers (+ detail drawer), Decisions, Monitoring (+ Model Risk), Data/Model, Settings.
Visitor mode: **Operate** — a risk analyst completing tasks; scanability, consistency, and the real desk scene outrank expression. Brand lives in precise detail.
Audience / job / action: credit-risk analyst or manager, mid-workday, needs portfolio health + concentration + who-needs-attention + why + what-action in ~10s, then drills into a segment, a borrower, or a policy threshold.
Proof / content: real pipeline aggregates (`../artifacts/*.json`, `../exports/portfolio_summary.parquet`) for every executive figure; the Decisions threshold simulator runs on the pipeline's real 60-row out-of-time backtest curve; row-level borrowers, per-decision attributions, and monthly series are seeded synthetic DEMO, badged on every surface.
Constraints: client-only React/Vite/TS, hand-built SVG charts, self-hosted variable font, WCAG AA, `prefers-reduced-motion`, no library that reads as a template.
Direction is **brief-pinned** (Apple design *principles*, not Apple UI) — no concept roll. Code-led (no image generation available).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A credit-risk console that reads like a well-set financial report, not a BI canvas — one calm reading column, risk carried by a single semantic colour scale that stays silent when nothing is wrong. It refuses the tiled-KPI-card grid, the rainbow chart, and the admin-panel chrome.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white cool ground (#FBFBFC) on #FFFFFF surfaces; ink #17191F, secondary #5C6270, tertiary #8B909C; 1px hairlines #E8E9ED, never a coloured bar. One family, Public Sans Variable, weight- and size-driven hierarchy (like a single-family system face); lining tabular numerals everywhere a figure can change. Risk scale and only this colour: Low #2F7D57, Medium #B07D1A, High #C0552C, Critical #9E2B22 — used as small fills, dots, and single data series, never as card backgrounds. Interactive affordance: one restrained slate-blue #33528C, hairline focus ring in the same hue. Cards are a 1px hairline + 12px radius + generous padding, no shadow except a 1px/2px soft ambient on the borrower drawer and popovers. 8px spacing system, section gaps ≥ 48px, comfortable 20–28px card padding.

STORY: The analyst opens Overview and within seconds reads: portfolio exposure and expected loss, whether risk is trending up or down, the risk-band spectrum, where loss concentrates (grade C–D), and 2–3 quiet "needs attention" items. They click a segment or a borrower to see why, or open Decisions to move the approval threshold and watch approvals ↔ expected loss ↔ profit trade off along the real backtest curve.

FIRST VIEWPORT (Overview): page title "Credit Risk" at ~34px/600 with one line of secondary supporting text; a single horizontal KPI row of five typographic units (figure ~28px tabular / label ~12px tertiary / one small delta with a 10px trend caret in a risk or neutral tint) sitting directly on the ground, no cards, generous gaps; immediately below, the dominant element — "Portfolio Risk Distribution", a full-width horizontal segmented risk spectrum (Low/Medium/High/Critical) with proportional widths, inline labels and share %, and a one-line read-out ("34% of exposure sits in Elevated or higher"). No hero card shell. Primary navigation is a quiet 200px left rail: wordmark, five items with 20px line icons, Settings + Data/Model at the foot.

FORM: single-column report layout with a fixed left rail; not on an ordered candidate list (brief-pinned direction, no roll). Seed key: none (pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
